# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q4 2023 vs Q4 2022

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

Saudi Arabia's point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) experienced a significant increase in Q4 2023 compared to Q4 2022, with a 9.7% increase in the average weekly POS value. Beverage and Food and Restaurants & Café saw their values increase by 1.07 billion SAR, while Miscellaneous Goods and Services experienced a 14.1% increase. The increase in Beverage and Food was driven by a 14.1% increase in average weekly value, while Restaurants & Café increased by 12.3%. The increase in Miscellaneous Goods and Services was driven by a 15.3% increase in average weekly value. The largest movers (by size of change) were Beverage and Food, Restaurants & Café, and Miscellaneous Goods and Services.
