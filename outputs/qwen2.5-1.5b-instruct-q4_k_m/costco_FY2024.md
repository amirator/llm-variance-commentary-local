# Costco Wholesale, FY2024 vs FY2023

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

Costco Wholesale Corporation reported a year-over-year increase in revenue of 5.0% to $254.5 billion in FY2024 compared to $242.3 billion in FY2023. Operating income also saw an increase of 14.4% to $9.29 billion, marking a $1.17 billion increase from $8.11 billion in FY2023. The company's largest revenue and income contributors were net income (+17.1%) and operating income (+14.4%).
