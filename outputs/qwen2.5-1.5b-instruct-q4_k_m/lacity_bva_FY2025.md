# LA City General Fund budget vs actual, FY2025

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

The City of Los Angeles's General Fund adopted budget for FY2025 was $9.38 billion, which was revised to $10.20 billion. During the fiscal year, there was a budget revision of $812.2 million, resulting in a utilisation rate of 93.9% for the revised budget. The lowest budget utilisation was observed in the Non-Departmental - Unappropriated Balance, at 100.0%, and in the Non-Departmental - General City Purposes, at 44.6%. The utilisation rate for the Council was 76.0%, and for the unspent budget, it was 24.0%. The unspent share of the revised budget was 6.1%, and the unspent budget was $626.6 million.
