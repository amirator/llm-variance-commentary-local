# LA City General Fund postings, July 2025 vs July 2024

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

The General Fund postings in July 2025 increased by 1.7% compared to July 2024, totaling $633.4 million. Non-Departmental - Liability Claims experienced a decrease of $30.9 million, while Police saw an increase of $16.5 million. Fire also showed an increase of $15.6 million. Non-Departmental - General City Purposes increased by $4.5 million.
