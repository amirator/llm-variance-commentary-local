# LA City General Fund postings, October 2025 vs October 2024

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In October 2025, the General Fund postings increased by 7.6% compared to the same month in 2024, totaling $780.9 million. This increase is attributed to the Non-Departmental - Capital Finance Administration, which saw an increase of $64.3 million, and the Fire department, which experienced a decrease of $21.4 million.
