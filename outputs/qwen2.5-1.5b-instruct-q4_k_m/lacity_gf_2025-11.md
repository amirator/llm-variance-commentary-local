# LA City General Fund postings, November 2025 vs November 2024

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In November 2025, the City of Los Angeles General Fund experienced a year-over-year increase of 2.2%, marking a total increase of $12.9 million from the November 2024 amount of $578.6 million. Notably, the largest changes were observed in the Fire department, with postings increasing by $13.6 million, and the Police department, with postings increasing by $6.4 million. Additionally, the Non-Departmental - Liability Claims category saw a decrease of $6.0 million, reflecting a reduction of 34.9% from the November 2024 figure. The Public Works - Street Services category also experienced a decrease of $5.1 million, marking a 26.7% decrease from the November 2024 figure. Lastly, the Non-Departmental - General City Purposes category saw an increase of $4.6 million, indicating a 45.1% increase from the November 2024 figure.
