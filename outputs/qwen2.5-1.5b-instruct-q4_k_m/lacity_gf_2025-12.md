# LA City General Fund postings, December 2025 vs December 2024

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In December 2025, the General Fund postings increased by 16.5% compared to the same month in 2024, reaching $737.8 million. This increase was driven by Non-Departmental - General, which saw an increase of $115.6 million to $177.9 million, and Fire, which decreased by $9.9 million to $79.0 million.
