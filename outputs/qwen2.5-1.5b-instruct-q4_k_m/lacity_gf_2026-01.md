# LA City General Fund postings, January 2026 vs January 2025

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In January 2026, the City of Los Angeles General Fund experienced a 1.6% increase in postings compared to the same month in 2025. This increase was driven by the Non-Departmental - Liability Claims, which saw a 443.3% increase in postings from $5.2 million to $28.3 million. Additionally, the Non-Departmental - Capital Finance Administration saw a 312.0% increase, with postings from $4.6 million to $19.0 million. These figures highlight significant growth in the city's financial operations.
