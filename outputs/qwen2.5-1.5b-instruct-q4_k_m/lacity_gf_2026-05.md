# LA City General Fund postings, May 2026 vs May 2025

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In May 2026, the General Fund postings decreased by 1.7% compared to the same month in 2025, totaling $871.1 million. This decrease is primarily attributed to the Fire department, which saw a decrease of $30.1 million from $67.6 million in May 2025, marking a 44.6% decrease. Additionally, the Non-Departmental - Liability Claims category experienced a decrease of $17.8 million from $27.9 million in May 2025, indicating a 63.8% decrease.
