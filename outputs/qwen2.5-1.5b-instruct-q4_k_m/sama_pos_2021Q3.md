# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q3 2021 vs Q3 2020

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In Q3 2021, the average weekly POS value increased by 24.0% compared to Q3 2020, marking a significant growth in the Saudi Arabia point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) system. This increase is attributed to the robust performance of the Restaurants & Café sector, which saw an increase of 33.7% in average weekly value. Additionally, the Gas Stations sector also experienced a notable rise of 114.0%, with an average weekly value of SAR 582 mn. The Miscellaneous Goods and Services sector also showed a positive trend, with an increase of 54.0% in average weekly value, reaching SAR 864 mn.
