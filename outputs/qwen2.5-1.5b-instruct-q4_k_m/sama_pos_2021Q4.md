# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q4 2021 vs Q4 2020

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In Q4 2021, the Saudi Arabia point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) saw an average weekly POS value of SAR 9.60 billion, marking a +19.5% increase from the same quarter in 2020. This growth is primarily attributed to the Restaurants & Café sector, which experienced a +28.9% increase in average weekly value, reaching SAR 1,356 million. Additionally, the Gas Stations sector also reported a +94.4% increase, totaling SAR 615 million.
