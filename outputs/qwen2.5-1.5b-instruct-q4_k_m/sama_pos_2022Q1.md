# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q1 2022 vs Q1 2021

Source: **llm** (2 model attempt(s), first attempt failed)

In the first quarter of 2022, the average weekly POS value in Saudi Arabia increased by 21.6% compared to the same period in 2021. This significant growth is attributed to the increased value of transactions in the Restaurants & Café sector, which saw an increase of 36.8% and an average weekly value of SAR 1,466 million. The Other sector also experienced a notable increase of 30.9%, with an average weekly value of SAR 1,515 million. The Jewelry sector, however, showed a decrease of 23.0%, with an average weekly value of SAR 208 million. The Electronic & Electric Devices sector also experienced a decrease of 12.2%, with an average weekly value of SAR 305 million. Despite these decreases, the overall POS value increased by SAR 1.85 billion, reflecting a strong performance in the quarter.
