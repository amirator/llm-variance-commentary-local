# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q3 2022 vs Q3 2021

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In Q3 2022, the Saudi Arabia point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) saw an average weekly value of SAR 10.85 billion, marking a +19.0% increase from Q3 2021, where the value was SAR 9.11 billion. The Other category, which includes miscellaneous goods and services, saw a +31.2% increase to SAR 1,628 million from SAR 1,241 million. The Miscellaneous Goods and Services category also reported a +38.0% increase to SAR 1,193 million from SAR 864 million. The Restaurants & Café category experienced a +23.3% increase to SAR 1,545 million from SAR 1,253 million, while the Electronic & Electric Devices category showed a -5.5% decrease to SAR 255 million from SAR 270 million. The Telecommunication category reported a -6.4% decrease to SAR 92 million from SAR 98 million.
