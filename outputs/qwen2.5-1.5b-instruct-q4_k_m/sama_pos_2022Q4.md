# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q4 2022 vs Q4 2021

Source: **llm** (2 model attempt(s), first attempt failed)

In Q4 2022, the average weekly POS value increased by 14.6% compared to the same quarter in 2021, reaching SAR 11.01 billion. This significant increase is attributed to the Other category, which saw a 25.0% increase in average weekly value, totaling SAR 1,679 million. The Miscellaneous Goods and Services category also experienced a notable increase, with a 29.3% rise, totaling SAR 1,219 million. These positive changes in Other and Miscellaneous Goods and Services indicate a strong performance in the retail sector.
