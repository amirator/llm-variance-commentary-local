# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q1 2023 vs Q1 2022

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In Q1 2023, the Saudi Arabia point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) showed a 10.0% increase compared to Q1 2022, marking a significant rise in the average weekly POS value from SAR 10.41 billion to SAR 11.45 billion. The largest movers in this quarter were Other, with a 20.9% increase in average weekly value from SAR 1.515 billion to SAR 1.831 billion, and Beverage and Food, with a 11.1% increase from SAR 1.643 billion to SAR 1.825 billion.
