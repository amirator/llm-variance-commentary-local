# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q1 2024 vs Q1 2023

Source: **llm** (2 model attempt(s), first attempt failed)

In Q1 2024, the average weekly POS value increased by 13.5% compared to the same period in 2023, reaching SAR 13.00 billion. This significant increase is attributed to the Miscellaneous Goods and Services category, which saw a rise of SAR 365 million, marking an 30.5% increase. The Beverage and Food category also experienced a notable growth, with an increase of SAR 239 million, representing a 13.1% increase.
