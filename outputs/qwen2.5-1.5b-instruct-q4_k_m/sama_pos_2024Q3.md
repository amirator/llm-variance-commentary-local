# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q3 2024 vs Q3 2023

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In Q3 2024, the Saudi Arabia point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) weekly statistics showed a significant increase of 8.4% compared to the same quarter in 2023. This is primarily attributed to the Miscellaneous Goods and Services category, which saw an increase of 18.6% in average weekly value, and Other, which also experienced a 11.5% increase. The rise in Miscellaneous Goods and Services is notable as it contributed to a total increase of 242 million Saudi Riyals (SAR) in average weekly value. The Other category also saw a notable increase of 221 million SAR, contributing to a total increase of 213 million SAR in average weekly value. These positive changes reflect an overall improvement in the spending habits of consumers, particularly in the sectors of Miscellaneous Goods and Services and Other.
