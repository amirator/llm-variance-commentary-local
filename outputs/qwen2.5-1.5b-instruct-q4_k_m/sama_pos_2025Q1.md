# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q1 2025 vs Q1 2024

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In Q1 2025, the Saudi Arabia point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) reported an average weekly POS value of SAR 13.95 billion, marking a +7.3% increase from Q1 2024. This significant rise is attributed to the increase in other and miscellaneous goods and services, which saw a +20.6% increase in average weekly value from SAR 2,056 million to SAR 2,480 million. Additionally, the clothing and footwear category also experienced an increase of +12.6% in average weekly value from SAR 1,050 million to SAR 1,183 million.
