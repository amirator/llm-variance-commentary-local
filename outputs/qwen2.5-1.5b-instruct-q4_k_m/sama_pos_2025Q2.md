# Saudi point-of-sale spending, Q2 2025 vs Q2 2024

Source: **llm** (1 model attempt(s), first attempt passed)

In Q2 2025, the Saudi Arabia point-of-sale card spending (SAMA) reached a total of SAR 12.88 billion, marking a 5.1% increase from the same period in 2024. This increase is primarily attributed to the Other category, which saw an increase of SAR 472 million, or 23.5%, from SAR 2,009 million in Q2 2024. The Gas Stations category also reported a 9.5% increase, with an average weekly value of SAR 924 million compared to SAR 844 million in 2024.
